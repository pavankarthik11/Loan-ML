# Loan Guide

## 1. What is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a bank or financial institution. The borrower agrees to repay the money over time along with interest.

---

## 2. Types of Loans

### Personal Loan
Used for personal expenses such as medical bills, travel, or emergencies.

### Home Loan
Used to purchase or construct a house.

### Education Loan
Used to pay tuition fees and other educational expenses.

### Business Loan
Used to start or expand a business.

---

## 3. Credit Score

A credit score represents how responsibly a person has managed previous loans and credit.

- 800 - 900 : Excellent
- 750 - 799 : Very Good
- 700 - 749 : Good
- 650 - 699 : Fair
- Below 650 : Poor

A higher credit score increases the chance of loan approval.

---

## 4. Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)

Debt-to-Income Ratio compares a person's monthly debt payments to their monthly income.

Formula:

DTI = Monthly Debt / Monthly Income

Lower DTI indicates better financial stability.

General guideline:

- Below 20% : Excellent
- 20% - 35% : Good
- Above 40% : High Risk

---

## 5. Interest Rate

Interest rate is the percentage charged by the lender for borrowing money.

Lower interest rates reduce the total repayment amount.

Higher interest rates increase the cost of the loan.

---

## 6. Loan Grades

Banks often assign loan grades.

Grade A:
Lowest risk borrowers.

Grade B:
Low risk borrowers.

Grade C:
Moderate risk.

Grade D:
Medium risk.

Grade E:
High risk.

Grade F:
Very high risk.

Grade G:
Highest risk.

---

## 7. Factors Affecting Loan Approval

Banks generally consider:

- Credit Score
- Annual Income
- Debt-to-Income Ratio
- Employment Status
- Loan Amount
- Interest Rate
- Previous repayment history

---

## 8. Documents Required

Common documents include:

- Government ID
- Address Proof
- Salary Slips
- Bank Statements
- Income Tax Returns
- Passport-size Photograph

---

## 9. Reasons for Loan Rejection

A loan application may be rejected because of:

- Poor credit score
- High debt
- Unstable employment
- Low income
- Missing documents
- Previous loan defaults

---

## 10. Tips to Improve Loan Approval

Applicants can improve their chances by:

- Paying bills on time
- Maintaining a good credit score
- Reducing existing debt
- Avoiding multiple loan applications
- Maintaining stable employment

---

## 11. EMI

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment.

It is the fixed monthly payment made to repay a loan.

EMI depends on:

- Loan Amount
- Interest Rate
- Loan Duration

---

## 12. Default Risk

Default means failing to repay the loan according to the agreed schedule.

Higher default risk may result from:

- Poor credit history
- High debt
- Low income
- Irregular employment

Banks try to minimize default risk before approving loans.
